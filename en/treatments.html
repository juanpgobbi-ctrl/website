Muscle relaxant

Wrinkles · teeth grinding · sweating · migraine

A muscle relaxant gently relaxes individual facial muscles. It is the treatment many people know as “Botox”. Expression lines soften while your face keeps its natural expression. The same principle also helps with teeth grinding, excessive sweating and chronic migraine.

  • Forehead lines, frown lines, crow’s feet
  • Bunny lines, chin, fine lines on the upper lip
  • Teeth grinding and tense jaw muscles (bruxism)
  • Excessive sweating of the underarms and hands (hyperhidrosis)
  • Chronic migraine: only after a diagnosis by a neurologist
  • Other areas: neck and jawline (platysma), corners of the mouth, trapezius and more
Onset 3–7 days
Duration 3–6 months
Session time approx. 30 min
Downtime Usually none

Fillers

Volume and contour

Fillers restore lost volume and define contours, for example on the lips, cheeks or chin. The result is visible immediately. Depending on the area and your goal, I work with one of two products.

  • Lips: shape, contour, subtle volume
  • Cheeks and cheekbones
  • Nasolabial folds and mouth corners
  • Chin and jawline
  • Non-surgical nose correction: bridge, tip and small asymmetries of the nose, depending on your anatomy
  • Tear trough: a tired-looking eye area

Hyaluronic acid

Occurs naturally in our skin. Soft and versatile, and it can be dissolved again if needed. Lasts 6–18 months, depending on the area.

Calcium hydroxylapatite

A firmer filler, suited above all to the chin, jawline and cheeks. It cannot be dissolved; the body breaks it down gradually over time. Usually lasts 12–18 months.

Onset Immediate
Final result After approx. 2 weeks
Duration 6–18 months, depending on area and product
Reversible Depends on the product

Injection lipolysis

Fat-dissolving injections

Small pockets of fat that stay despite exercise and diet, such as a double chin, can be reduced with targeted injections. The body gradually clears the treated fat cells. With a stable weight the result can last a long time; how visible it is varies from person to person.

  • Double chin: the most common use
  • Small, localised fat pockets
  • Several sessions are usually needed
  • No surgery, no anaesthesia
  • The area is swollen for a few days afterwards
Visible results After 4–8 weeks
Sessions Usually 2–6
Session time approx. 30 min
Downtime Swelling, usually a few days

Biostimulators

Stimulating collagen, not volume

Biostimulators encourage the skin to produce more of its own collagen. They add little volume but can make the skin firmer, plumper and fresher over time. The effect develops slowly over weeks.

Skin booster (hyaluronic acid)

Fluid hyaluronic acid that spreads through the skin. For more hydration and elasticity; usually two sessions about one month apart.

Poly-L-lactic acid

Stimulates collagen over months. For gradual tightening when the face has lost volume.

Calcium hydroxylapatite, diluted

Highly diluted, it adds no volume but firms the skin, for example on the cheeks, neck, décolletage or hands.

Salmon DNA (polynucleotides)

May support the regeneration of thin, delicate skin, for example under the eyes and on the neck.

Results Gradually, over weeks
Duration 6–24 months, depending on the product
Sessions Usually 2–4
Downtime Minimal

Mesotherapy

Vitamins for the skin

A mix of vitamins, amino acids and hyaluronic acid is delivered directly into the skin. This can improve hydration and give the skin a fresher look.

  • Skin quality and radiance
  • More hydration
  • Prevention of early signs of skin ageing
  • Scalp: supportive option for hair loss
  • Mix adapted to the skin’s condition

Important information

Before any treatment we talk in detail about your goals, your health, the risks and side effects, and what can realistically be achieved given your anatomy. I only treat when it is medically sensible and safe.

Every person is unique. I adapt the treatment to your skin type, anatomy and age, and results can vary from person to person.

Treatments are billed privately under the German fee schedule for physicians (Gebührenordnung für Ärzte, GOÄ). You will know the expected cost before we start.

I only use approved medicines and CE-certified medical devices.

In line with the German Therapeutic Products Advertising Act (HWG), this website shows no before-and-after images. I am happy to answer your questions about possible results in person.

Treatment

Muscle relaxant

How often

Usually every 3–6 months, when movement starts to come back. Muscle strength, metabolism and the area treated all play a part, and some people build up a tolerance and need higher doses or shorter intervals. For teeth grinding and sweating the effect often lasts a little longer.

The first hours

You can go back to your day. The injection sites may stay visible for an hour or two, and in some cases there may even be a small haematoma. The full effect shows after 2 weeks: only then can it be assessed and, if necessary, small corrections made.

Aftercare

  • Stay upright for 4 hours
  • Do not massage the area or do intense exercise on the same day
  • Avoid sauna and direct sun for 48 hours

Treatment

Fillers

How often

There are no preset intervals. Different products and amounts lose their effect at different speeds, and further treatments are discussed according to when they are needed.

One session or several

One session is often enough. For larger changes I prefer to build up over two sessions a few weeks apart, so that the result stays in proportion.

The first days

Right after treatment the area is red and may look more swollen. Swelling and small haematomas can last several days and fade on their own. After 2 weeks the filler has settled: only then can it be assessed.

Good to know

If you often get cold sores, please let me know beforehand.

Treatment

Injection lipolysis

How many sessions

Usually 2–6, every 3–8 weeks. How many you need depends on the size of the fat pocket and how your body responds.

The first days

Plan for it: the area is clearly swollen and tender, sometimes with haematomas. This usually settles within a few days, generally within a week, so it is best not to schedule a session right before something important.

Afterwards

You can judge the result 4–8 weeks after a session. Usually no regular maintenance is needed as long as your weight stays stable.

Treatment

Biostimulators

How often

Usually 2–4 sessions, every 4–8 weeks. Top-ups as needed.

Why more or fewer sessions

It depends on many factors. For example, thinner or more sun-damaged skin usually needs more sessions, and not every product is given at the same rhythm.

The first hours and days

The injection sites may stay visible for several hours, sometimes until the next day, and in some cases there may even be a small haematoma.

Good to know

Collagen builds slowly, so it is worth judging the result after a few months, not after a few days.

Treatment

Mesotherapy

How often

3–5 sessions to begin with, every 3–8 weeks, then a session every 3–6 months if you want to maintain the effect.

Why more or fewer sessions

It depends on the condition of your skin and on your goal. First effects usually appear after 1–3 sessions.

The first hours

There is no real recovery time, but your face will be red for a few hours and the injection sites can stay visible until the next day. It is best not to plan anything important for the same day.

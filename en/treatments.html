Muscle relaxant

Wrinkles · teeth grinding · sweating · migraine

A muscle relaxant gently relaxes individual facial muscles. Expression lines soften while your face keeps its natural expression. The same principle also helps with teeth grinding, excessive sweating and chronic migraine.

  • Forehead lines, frown lines, crow’s feet
  • Bunny lines, chin, fine lines on the upper lip
  • Teeth grinding and tense jaw muscles (bruxism)
  • Excessive sweating of the underarms and hands (hyperhidrosis)
  • Chronic migraine: only after a diagnosis by a neurologist
  • Other areas: neck and jawline (platysma), corners of the mouth, trapezius and more
Onset 3–7 days
Duration 3–6 months
Session time approx. 30 min
Downtime Usually none

Fillers

Volume and contour

Fillers restore volume and define contours. Two types are used, with different properties.

Hyaluronic acid

Hyaluronic acid occurs naturally in our skin. As a filler it can restore lost volume and define contours, for example on the lips, cheeks or chin. The result is visible immediately and can be dissolved again if needed.

  • Lips: shape, contour, subtle volume
  • Cheeks and cheekbones
  • Nasolabial folds and mouth corners
  • Chin and jawline
  • Non-surgical nose correction: bridge, tip and small asymmetries of the nose, depending on your anatomy
  • Tear trough: a tired-looking eye area
Onset Immediate
Final result After approx. 2 weeks
Duration 6–18 months, depending on the area
Reversible Yes

Calcium hydroxylapatite

A firmer filler, suited above all to the chin, jawline and cheeks. Unlike hyaluronic acid it cannot be dissolved; the body breaks it down gradually over time.

Duration 12–18 months
Reversible No (cannot be dissolved)

Injection lipolysis

Fat-dissolving injections

Small pockets of fat that stay despite exercise and diet, such as a double chin, can be reduced with targeted injections. The body gradually clears the treated fat cells. With a stable weight the result can last a long time; how visible it is varies from person to person.

  • Double chin: the most common use
  • Small, localised fat pockets
  • Several sessions are usually needed
  • No surgery, no anaesthesia
  • The area is swollen for a few days afterwards
Visible results After 4–8 weeks
Sessions Usually 2–6
Session time approx. 30 min
Downtime Swelling, usually a few days

Biostimulators

Stimulating collagen, not volume

Biostimulators encourage the skin to produce more of its own collagen. They add little volume but can make the skin firmer, plumper and fresher over time. The effect develops slowly over weeks.

Hyaluronic acid booster

Fluid hyaluronic acid that spreads through the skin. For more hydration and elasticity; usually two sessions about one month apart.

Poly-L-lactic acid

Stimulates collagen over months. For gradual tightening when the face has lost volume.

Calcium hydroxylapatite, diluted

Highly diluted, it adds no volume but firms the skin, for example on the cheeks, neck, décolletage or hands.

Salmon DNA (polynucleotides)

Also known as “salmon sperm”. May support the regeneration of thin, delicate skin, for example under the eyes and on the neck.

Results Gradually, over weeks
Duration 6–24 months, depending on the product
Sessions Usually 2–4
Downtime Minimal

Mesotherapy

Vitamin skin booster

A mix of vitamins, amino acids and hyaluronic acid is delivered directly into the skin. This can improve hydration and give the skin a fresher look. Also known as “skin booster”.

  • Skin quality and radiance
  • More hydration
  • Prevention of early signs of skin ageing
  • Scalp: supportive option for hair loss
  • Mix adapted to the skin’s condition
Results After 1–3 sessions
Sessions 3–5 initially
Maintenance Every 3–6 months
Downtime Usually none

Treatment

Muscle relaxant

Relaxes overactive muscles and softens expression lines without making your face look stiff. I adapt the dose to your muscles.

Common indications

  • Forehead, frown lines and crow’s feet
  • Teeth grinding and jaw pain
  • Excessive sweating
  • Chronic migraine, after a neurological diagnosis

What to expect

The effect starts after 3–7 days and is complete after about two weeks. It usually lasts 3–6 months. You can go straight back to your day.

Aftercare

  • Stay upright for 4 hours
  • Do not massage the area or do intense exercise on the same day
  • Avoid sauna and direct sun for 48 hours

Fillers

Fillers restore volume and define contours. Two types are used, with different properties.

Hyaluronic acid

Hyaluronic acid restores lost volume and defines contours. You see the result immediately; after about two weeks the filler has fully settled.

  • Lips
  • Cheeks
  • Chin and jawline
  • Non-surgical nose and tear trough

Mild swelling or small bruises are normal in the first days and fade on their own. Depending on the area the result lasts 6–18 months. If you often get cold sores, please let me know beforehand.

Calcium hydroxylapatite

A firmer filler for the chin, jawline and cheeks. It usually lasts 12–18 months and cannot be dissolved.

Treatment

Injection lipolysis

Reduces small fat pockets with injections, without surgery. The body gradually clears the treated fat cells.

Common indications

  • Double chin
  • Small, localised fat pockets

What to expect

Afterwards the area is swollen and a little tender. This usually settles within a few days, generally within a week. You see the result after 4–8 weeks, usually after several sessions.

Good to know

Not suitable during pregnancy and breastfeeding. We discuss everything else in the consultation.

Biostimulators

Biostimulators encourage the skin to produce more of its own collagen. They add little volume and improve skin quality gradually.

Options

  • Hyaluronic acid booster: hydration and elasticity
  • Poly-L-lactic acid: gradual tightening
  • Diluted calcium hydroxylapatite: firmer skin
  • Salmon DNA: regeneration of thin skin

What to expect

The effect builds up over weeks and lasts 6–24 months depending on the product. Usually 2–4 sessions a few weeks apart, then a top-up once or twice a year.

I’m Juan Gobbi, a physician trained in Buenos Aires and practising in Berlin for over five years. Several years in general and plastic surgery, and today intensive care, shape how I approach aesthetic treatments: anatomically informed and restrained.

The goal is not to change you, but to enhance what is there, naturally and in balance.

Treatments

Medicine and aesthetics, in measure.

Wrinkle treatment

Forehead, frown lines, crow’s feet: relaxed muscles and a rested look, without a frozen expression.

Lip fillers

Subtle volume and a clear contour with hyaluronic acid, in proportion to your face.

Skin rejuvenation

Your skin is encouraged to make its own collagen and can look firmer and fresher over time, without changing your face.

Teeth grinding

Relaxing the jaw muscle for bruxism and jaw pain. The face often looks a little slimmer afterwards.

Non-surgical nose job

Smoothing small irregularities of the nasal bridge with hyaluronic acid, without surgery.

Fat-dissolving injections

For small fat pockets such as a double chin, without surgery. Long-lasting with a stable weight.

Skin booster

Vitamins and hyaluronic acid delivered into the skin for more hydration and freshness.

Migraine

Treatment for chronic migraine, only after a neurological diagnosis and with a medical indication. We discuss it in the consultation.

Salmon DNA

Also known as “salmon sperm”. Supports the regeneration of thin skin under the eyes and on the neck.

Common questions

What you should know.

How does my first appointment work?

Every treatment starts with a detailed consultation. I only treat once we have weighed the options together and there is a medical indication.

Will the results look natural?

That is my main goal: no exaggerated volume, no frozen expression, but changes that look as natural as possible.

Do you also treat men?

Yes, many of my patients are men. I adapt the treatment to their anatomy and their wishes.

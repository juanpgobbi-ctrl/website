Medical expertise meets
aesthetic sensibility.

The most sophisticated aesthetics are the ones you don't notice.

I'm Juan Gobbi, a physician trained in Buenos Aires and based in Berlin for over five years. I worked in general and plastic surgery and now work in anaesthesia and intensive care medicine.

That clinical training shapes the way I understand aesthetic medicine: from a deep knowledge of anatomy and sound medical judgement.

Since 2022 I have focused on aesthetic medicine, centred on injectable treatments and minimally invasive procedures. I work mainly with muscle relaxants, hyaluronic acid and biostimulators, prioritising subtle results that respect each person's expression and identity.

I aim to enhance what is already there, in harmony with your own features.

What you should know
before your treatment.

Good preparation is part of a good treatment. Here is what matters most for your appointment.

General advice

  • Please come without make-up
  • Drink plenty of water on the day of your appointment
  • No alcohol for 24 hours beforehand
  • Tell me which medication you take and whether you have any allergies
  • Plan a filler about two weeks before an important event: that is how long it takes to settle fully
  • Avoid dental treatment and vaccinations in the two weeks before and after a filler if possible

Blood thinners & medication

  • Avoid painkillers such as ibuprofen or aspirin for 3–5 days beforehand, unless prescribed by a doctor
  • Omega-3 (fish oil), high-dose vitamin E or ginkgo can make bruising more likely; pause them a week before if possible
  • Do not stop blood pressure medication or prescribed blood thinners on your own
  • If in doubt, just ask beforehand

When I do not treat

  • Pregnancy and breastfeeding
  • Acute infection or inflammation at the treatment site
  • Fever or acute illness
  • Clotting disorders
  • Recurring cold sores, known allergies or a previous reaction to a filler: please tell me beforehand
  • Certain autoimmune or neuromuscular diseases: please discuss beforehand

After treatment

  • No exercise on the day of treatment
  • Avoid sauna, sunbeds and direct sun for 48 hours
  • Do not rub or massage the treated area
  • After a muscle relaxant, stay upright for 4 hours
  • Mild swelling and small bruises are normal and fade on their own

This information is general. What applies to your treatment exactly is something we go through in the consultation.

When to get in touch

Please get in touch with me if, after a treatment:

  • the pain becomes strong or increases
  • the skin turns noticeably pale or bluish
  • redness, warmth or swelling get worse after a few days instead of settling

Reactions like these are rare. After every treatment I give you a phone number where you can reach me.
